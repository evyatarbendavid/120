# Pre-publish checklist

Go through it for every video. For each line, either tick it with evidence (a snapshot, a
measured number) or say what is still open.

## Technical
- [ ] The source file is unchanged (compare the hash).
- [ ] Final video is the intended size (1080x1920 for a reel).
- [ ] Loudness measured on the final file: about -14 LUFS, true peak at most -1 dBTP.
- [ ] The whole transcript was read, every sentence marked as checked.
- [ ] Every correction was approved by the user.
- [ ] The font loads in every scene, not just the first.
- [ ] Automatic checks (lint, structure) pass.
- [ ] The first frame looks good even as a still thumbnail, possibly blurred.
- [ ] The last frame is not cut in the middle of an animation.

## Transcript and captions
- [ ] Names, numbers and English terms are written correctly.
- [ ] Every caption is one complete phrase of at most two lines.
- [ ] No line is too wide for the safe area; none is split awkwardly.
- [ ] No caption flickers; each stays briefly after its last word.
- [ ] SRT and VTT match the on-screen captions of the final version.
- [ ] Captions cover neither the face nor any platform interface area.

## Graphics and motion
- [ ] Every event has a purpose tied to a sentence.
- [ ] Each object enters on its word, not before it.
- [ ] Zooms are centred, quick, and return sharply.
- [ ] Full-screen scenes are short and full of motion.
- [ ] No fake logos and no product screenshots that were not supplied.
- [ ] Illustrations have no halo and no clipped edges.
- [ ] Nothing important sits in a platform UI zone.

## Audio
- [ ] Speech is at full level and clear.
- [ ] Music is audible but does not compete with the voice.
- [ ] Quiet speech was raised before the music was mixed in.
- [ ] No distortion or clipping.

## Before publishing
- [ ] Watched the draft on a phone, once with sound and once without. Most people watch silently.
- [ ] The first seconds make the point of the video clear.
- [ ] Lessons from this video were written into the rules or design file.
