# Closing deals safely

Everything here gets prepared by Claude and approved by the owner. Nothing is sent, ordered or
paid without the owner's yes for that exact action.

## Supplier vetting checklist

Score each item as yes, no or unknown, and list the unknowns as questions for the supplier.

- Years on the platform, verified or audited badge, Trade Assurance (or an equivalent buyer
  protection)
- Rating and review count, and recent reviews that mention this product
- Response rate and time, reorder rate
- A factory or a trading company. Ask for a factory video or a short video call.
- Sample policy: the price, and whether the sample cost is refunded on the bulk order
- Certificates the market needs (CE, FCC, battery MSDS), when the product requires them
- Payment through the platform only. Never pay a supplier off-platform by wire transfer to a
  personal account.

## Scam and trouble signs: stop and warn the owner

- A price far below every other supplier
- Pressure to pay outside the platform, or a change of bank details by email
- No sample allowed, or "pay full amount first, we ship tomorrow"
- A new account with perfect reviews, or a stolen product photo (check with a reverse image search)
- A brand-name product at a generic price: likely counterfeit
- For a local deal: a seller who will not meet or show the item, a price too good to be true,
  "deposit to hold it", or a courier-only payment link

## Inquiry message template (adapt the product and quantities)

```
Hello <name>, we are a small online store in <country> looking at your <product> (<link>).
Could you share:
1. Price per unit at 100 / 300 / 1,000 pcs
2. MOQ, and whether a mixed-colour first order is possible
3. Sample price and shipping to <country>, and whether it is refunded on the first order
4. Lead time and the best shipping option to <country> (air / express / sea), with a cost estimate
5. Custom logo or packaging options, and their MOQ
6. Certificates available (CE / FCC / MSDS)
Payment through <platform> Trade Assurance. Thank you!
```

## Negotiation playbook

1. Collect at least three quotes first. Competing offers are the strongest argument.
2. Open below the target, about 15-20% under the best quote, with a reason (the volume plan, a
   repeat order).
3. Trade instead of just asking:
   - a larger order for a lower price;
   - a faster payment for a better tier;
   - a free sample or free logo for committing to the first order.
4. Get everything in writing on the platform: price, specs, packaging, lead time, defect policy.
5. Walk away when the price is above the walk-away number from `biz.py`, where margin falls under
   30%.

## Before money moves (the owner approves each step)

1. Sample order (small amount): the owner approves the amount and the method.
2. Check the sample: quality, the real shipping time, packaging. Take photos and short videos for
   the marketing.
3. Small first order, or a pre-sale test: a landing page and a small ad budget the owner approves,
   to check demand before stocking.
4. Bulk order only after the test shows a profit at the real costs.
